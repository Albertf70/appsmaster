Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Can I try the app for free?
Yes. V1 Basic with 2,588 words (A1-A2 level) is completely free. No credit card required.
How does the subscription work?
You pay $299 MXN (~$17 USD) once a year and V2, V3, and V4 are unlocked immediately. After payment you receive a license key by email that you enter in the app.
Can I cancel?
Yes, you can cancel at any time from your LemonSqueezy account. You keep access until your paid period ends.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The app is a PWA (Progressive Web App) that installs on your phone and works 100% offline. All vocabulary is included in the app.
What payment methods do you accept?
Through LemonSqueezy we accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal.
